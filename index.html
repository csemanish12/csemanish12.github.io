Selected work

The problem, what I did, and the result.

AI security · Founding engineer

KIPIO

Problem: teams adopting AI tools risk leaking sensitive data through prompts and file uploads.

What I did: built the endpoint DLP agent as primary author, and rebuilt the policy engine as a standalone service on GCP.

Result: an agent that enforces admin-defined data policies on employee devices in real time.

Connectivity · Backend & leadership

Elsight

Problem: drones and robots operating beyond visual line of sight can't afford to lose their link to the ground station. Halo keeps them connected by combining cellular, radio and satellite into one secure connection.

What I did: built backend services across Halo's cloud fleet-management services and the software on each device, including integrating a newer 4G modem from our modem provider and per-device usage metering for billing. Grew to Team Lead, also acting as tech lead and Scrum Master, and led work such as the device telemetry pipeline.

Result: Halo gained support for newer 4G modem hardware, billing is based on actual connection time, and features shipped end to end with product, QA, DevOps and support.

Healthcare · E-health

Jeevee Health

Problem: Jeevee's goal was to digitize health services in Nepal, from booking a doctor to buying health products, so patients no longer had to do everything in person.

What I did: built the core e-health backend from the initial design phase: online appointments for patients, doctor and schedule management for clinics, and vaccination bookings. Also built ordering and delivery for health products, payment integrations, and a blood request system that notifies matching donors.

Result: patients could book clinic and vaccination appointments online, order health products for delivery, and find matching blood donors, all on one platform.
